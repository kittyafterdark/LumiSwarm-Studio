Curation Verdict

No Candidates Selected in this Section

All previously tested cards in this family were reviewed and rejected (“they all suck”). Floating rectangular bubbles and detached side-by-side text plates dilute character presence and fail the visual integration standard. This section remains reserved as a target specification for future explorations that genuinely fuse dialogue into character silhouette.
